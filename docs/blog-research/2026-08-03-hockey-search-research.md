## 2. Utah — local Google + YouTube education demand

- **Utah hockey history (this week's Utah-local pick):** Verified via KSL Sports, KSLNewsRadio, NBC/Wikipedia: pro hockey in Salt Lake City dates to 1969 with the Salt Lake Golden Eagles (25 seasons across WHL/CHL/IHL, 3 Adams Cups, 2 Turner Cups, played at the Salt Palace/Maverik Center). The Utah Grizzlies followed in 1995 at the Delta Center, won a Turner Cup in 1996 in front of 17,381 fans (a minor-league attendance record at the time). The NHL arrived in April 2024 when the Arizona Coyotes relocated to Salt Lake City ($1.2B sale), later renamed the Utah Mammoth. This is a strong, differentiated, no-duplicate topic — nobody else is telling the full arc from Golden Eagles → Grizzlies → Mammoth → Glizzies beer league boom.
- **Utah Mammoth (NHL), for context/mentions only — not a new post:** Mammoth matched a 1-year/$4.775M offer sheet to keep Barrett Hayton; post-draft signings include Sebastian Cossa (2yr/$4M, acquired from Detroit), Yamamoto (2yr/$3.5M), and Nick DeSimone (2yr/$2M). Two seasons in, the Mammoth have sold out every game. Worth a passing mention in the history post's closing section, not enough for a standalone article this week (already covered contextually in utah-mammoth-changed-utah-hockey.html).
