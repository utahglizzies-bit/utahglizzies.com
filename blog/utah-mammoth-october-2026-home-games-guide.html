Utah Mammoth October 2026 Home Games: A Fan Guide

October 12, 2026 • 5 min read • By Utah Glizzies HC

The Utah Mammoth opened their 2026-27 season at the Delta Center on October 1 against Chicago, and then did what NHL teams do: left town for a long road trip. Home games are back this week. If you're a new fan (or a lapsed one), here's your October game-night cheat sheet, plus how to turn "I love this sport" into "I have a jersey and a questionable hockey bag."

Mammoth Home Games This Month

According to the published schedule, the Mammoth have five home dates left in October. Puck drops are Mountain Time, and schedules can shift, so double-check with the team before you buy tickets or book a sitter.

  • Tue, Oct. 13: vs. Toronto, 7 p.m.
  • Sat, Oct. 17: vs. Edmonton, 8 p.m.
  • Mon, Oct. 19: vs. Pittsburgh, 7 p.m.
  • Sat, Oct. 24: vs. Tampa Bay, 7 p.m.
  • Tue, Oct. 27: vs. Minnesota, 7 p.m.

Right after that, the team starts a stretch of six straight road games (Oct. 29 through Nov. 11), so the late-October homestand is your last local look for a bit.

Why 84 Games Changes the Vibe

This is the NHL's first 84-game regular season in more than 30 years. Two extra games means a longer grind, more tired legs by March, and more nights where a random Tuesday matchup matters. For fans, it also means more chances to catch a game live without selling a kidney.

Can't Make It? Watch Smart

Not every game needs a ticket. Our guide to where to watch hockey in Salt Lake City covers bars, watch parties, and couch strategy. And if you want the backstory on how a state with almost no NHL history turned into a sellout machine, read how the Utah Mammoth turned Utah into a hockey state.

Watching the Mammoth is how most Utah hockey players start. Nobody walks out of the Delta Center and says, "I'd like to be worse at that, on purpose, on Thursday nights." But here we are.
