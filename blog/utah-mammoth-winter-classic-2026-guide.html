Utah Mammoth Winter Classic 2026: Your Fan Guide

November 10, 2026 • 5 min read • By Utah Glizzies HC

Every so often the NHL takes the best indoor sport on earth and plops it in a football stadium in the freezing cold. On Thursday, December 31, 2026, that stadium is Rice-Eccles in Salt Lake City, the Utah Mammoth are the hosts, and the Colorado Avalanche are the guests. New Year's Eve hockey, outdoors, in Utah. Here's the beer-league-approved guide.

The Basics

Dec 31
Thursday, New Year's Eve 2026
4 PM
MT start per most ticket listings (one resale site shows 7 PM, so verify)
Rice-Eccles
University of Utah campus, not the Delta Center

This is the one extra home game on top of the Mammoth's regular 41 at the Delta Center. Start time and ticket rules can change at the league's discretion, so check the official ticket page before you plan your day. Resale listings we saw started around $240 and up, and prices move fast.

What to Wear (Rec Players Already Know This)

If you've ever played a 10:30 PM game in a rink where the heat is a rumor, you're ahead of everyone. The rule is layers:

  • Base layer: thermal or moisture-wicking. Cotton is a trap.
  • Mid layer: fleece or wool.
  • Outer layer: insulated and windproof. Stadium wind is real.
  • Extremities: wool socks, gloves, a hat. Hand warmers are the MVP.

Getting There

Game-day traffic around the U is no joke on a normal Saturday, and this is a holiday-week game with a national TV audience. Plan on transit, rideshare drop-offs a few blocks away, or parking well ahead. Check official Mammoth and University channels for gate times and transportation details, because those get published closer to the date.

Then Go Play Some Hockey Yourself

Outdoor hockey makes everybody want a pond. Utah doesn't have many frozen ponds you'd trust, but it does have indoor rinks, adult learn-to-skate classes, and open stick-and-puck sessions. Start with our Utah stick and puck guide or the adult learn-to-skate programs list. Already skating? The beer league joining guide gets you on a roster.

Want the rest of the Mammoth calendar? Our November home games guide covers the homestand.

Rice-Eccles Is Cold. The Sandy Rink Is Warmer.

The Mammoth play the Winter Classic outdoors. The Utah Glizzies play at the Utah Mammoth Ice Center in Sandy, indoors, with hot dogs. Come meet the team.
