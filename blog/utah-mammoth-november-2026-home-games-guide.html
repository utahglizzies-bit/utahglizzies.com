Utah Mammoth November 2026 Home Games: A Fan Guide

October 19, 2026 • 5 min read • By Utah Glizzies HC

The Mammoth are about to leave town for a long road trip, then come home and stay home. After six straight road games (Oct. 29 through Nov. 11), the team returns to the Delta Center for a five-game homestand, the second-longest of the season. Here's your November game-night cheat sheet, plus how to turn "I love this sport" into "I own a hockey bag that smells like regret."

The November Home Schedule

All six November home games are at the Delta Center in downtown Salt Lake City. Times are Mountain Time.

  • Thu, Nov. 12: vs. St. Louis Blues, 7:00 p.m.
  • Sat, Nov. 14: vs. San Jose Sharks, 7:00 p.m.
  • Mon, Nov. 16: vs. Vancouver Canucks, 7:30 p.m.
  • Wed, Nov. 18: vs. Colorado Avalanche, 7:00 p.m.
  • Sat, Nov. 21: vs. Anaheim Ducks, 7:00 p.m.
  • Mon, Nov. 30: vs. Montreal Canadiens, 7:30 p.m.

Schedules move. Double-check dates and puck drops on the official Mammoth schedule before you buy tickets or book a babysitter.

Which Game Should You Pick?

If you only go to one, make it the Colorado game on Nov. 18. Division rivals, loud building, and a midweek night when nobody has to explain to their boss why they're hoarse. The Saturday games against San Jose (Nov. 14) and Anaheim (Nov. 21) are the easiest on the calendar for a first-timer. Montreal on Nov. 30 is the one for people who like their hockey history with a side of fan-base noise.

A five-game homestand is the hockey equivalent of an all-you-can-eat hot dog buffet. Pace yourself, hydrate, and do not eat a fifth one.

Game-Night Tips for New Fans

  1. Get there early. Warmups are half the show, and downtown parking and TRAX both get busy.
  2. Learn the three-second rule of icing. Okay, it isn't a three-second rule. Just ask the person next to you; hockey fans love explaining icing.
  3. Pick a player to watch. Following one skater all game teaches you more about positioning than any TV broadcast. Our positions guide helps.
  4. Can't make it? Check our list of where to watch hockey in Salt Lake City.
